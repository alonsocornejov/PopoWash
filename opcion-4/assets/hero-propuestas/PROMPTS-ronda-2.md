Use case: product-mockup. Generate one standalone professional photographic hero banner, ultra-wide 2.7:1. Inputs: image1 previous water campaign is a dynamism/composition reference ONLY; image2 original white PopoWash mechanical bidet is authoritative for exact product identity. Re-photograph the SAME bidet in an unmistakably left-leaning pose: rotate object counterclockwise 25 degrees from upright, upper mounting plate leaning to LEFT, knob pointing down-right; camera subtly from left. Do NOT mirror logo or swap the physical components. Preserve curved arm, TWO slotted circular mounting discs, chrome knob ring, red off mark, twin nozzle housing, white plastic and small printed POPO WASH mark. Whole product visible on RIGHT 58% with 8% edge clearance; LEFT 38% remains calm dark negative space for live HTML heading. Expensive restrained studio photography with huge softbox, nuanced white highlights and subtle shadows, realistic translucent water with silvery reflections, high-speed flash droplets. Palette relates to electric blue #3155ff and ink #111318 but deep and desaturated; no neon lime floodlight, no green haze, no cheap CGI plastic glow. Original small product logo only; NO headlines, UI, border, watermark, extra devices, hand or bathroom. Water is an abstract surrounding advertising element, not spray from controls.
